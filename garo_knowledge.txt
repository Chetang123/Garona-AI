GARО LANGUAGE KNOWLEDGE

IMPORTANT:
The Garo Tutor must use the Garo language for final answers when the user asks for an explanation in Garo.

Do not invent Garo words.
If the tutor is unsure about a translation, it should say that it is unsure.

====================
BASIC SENTENCES
====================

English: What is your name?
Garo: [Nangko mai minga?]

English: My name is Chetang.
Garo: [Angni bimung Chetang.]

English: How are you?
Garo: [Namengama?]

English: I am fine.
Garo: [Anga namenga]

English: What are you doing?
Garo: [Na.a mai dakenga]

English: I am working.
Garo: [Anga kam ka.enga.]
